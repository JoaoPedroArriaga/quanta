# QUANTA MOD - IMPLANTS

> **Document based on QUANTA_MASTERLIST v10.6**
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-16
> **Scope:** Cybernetic implants, implant components, and Cyber Chamber

## 🎯 OVERVIEW

**Cybernetic implants** are **permanent** upgrades installed in the player's body. Unlike armor, they **do not occupy slots** and **cannot be removed** (only deactivated).

**Philosophy:** players install implants in the **Cyber Chamber** (3×3×3 multiblock). Each implant is **permanent**, **passive**, and **free** once installed.

## 🏗️ 3. CYBER CHAMBER (MULTIBLOCK 3×3×3)

### 3.1 Structure

- **Base:** 3×3 of Singular Casing.
- **Walls:** Singular Glass Panel (transparent).
- **Ceiling:** Singular Casing with a 1×1 opening (entrance).
- **Controller:** block in the center of the front wall.
- **Fluid ports:** Chaos Gas (left) + Order Gas (right).

### 3.2 How to use

1. Put the gases in the tanks (Chaos + Order).
2. Enter the chamber (the door opens as you approach).
3. Place the implant components in the Controller slot.
4. The Controller consumes Q + gases and installs the implant.
5. Animation: purple/cyan particles circulate around your body.
6. You leave with the implant installed.

### 3.3 Rules

- **Without the complete structure:** it does not work.
- **Breaking a wall during installation:** 50% chance of losing components.
- **Each implant consumes:** 500–10,000 Q + 100 mB Chaos Gas + 100 mB Order Gas.
