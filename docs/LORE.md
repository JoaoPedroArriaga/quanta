# OVERVIEW

This document contains the complete lore and concepts of the Quanta Mod.

---

# WHAT IS QUANTA?

Quanta is a particle with information-altering properties;
by controlling quanta, you can rewrite reality.

---

# WHO ARE YOU?

To some, you are a mad scientist; to others, a brilliant mage. That hardly matters.
What really matters is that you are the first to discover quanta.
During your experiments in the End, using an Ender Pearl and the void, you found
this particle and now seek to study it and discover all its capabilities.

The discovery of Quanta in the End was accidental—but the particle had already
existed in trace concentrations in the Overworld. You start with Ender Pearls (in
the Overworld) before reaching the End. The End is where the concentration is high
enough for large-scale experiments.

# WHAT ARE QUANTA MACHINES?

Machines that manipulate quanta to process materials, generate energy, and conduct
experiments. Each machine can operate in two modes: **Order** (stable, safe) or
**Chaos** (unstable, powerful). The choice between order and chaos determines the
risk and reward.

An Assembly is an experiment built in the world: a Quantum Emitter projects a
straight beam of Quanta (up to 10 blocks). Modifiers alter the beam along its path.
At the end, a Quantum Receptor applies the modified beam to a material. An Assembly
Core anchors the setup.

Pre-assembled machines are "black box" versions of assemblies. Building your own
is cheaper, more flexible, and—above all—it's doing science.

A **Quantum Cyber Chamber** is a 3×3×3 chamber that the player enters to install
cybernetic implants. It is the only place where Quanta and biology fuse. Entering
the chamber is symbolic: you leave different from how you entered.

# WHAT ARE EQUATIONS?

These are formulas written on the **Black Board** and stored in the **Quantum
Codex**. They combine a **Form** (Self, Projectile, Touch, Area), an **Element**
(Order, Chaos, Singularity), and an **Effect** (Damage, Heal, Teleport, etc.).
Casting an Equation consumes Quanta and can alter the surrounding reality.

# WHAT ARE IMPLANTS?

Permanent cybernetic upgrades installed in the **Quantum Cyber Chamber**. Once
installed, they are passive and free, making the player increasingly powerful.
There is no limit to how many implants a player can have—the goal is to transcend
humanity itself.
