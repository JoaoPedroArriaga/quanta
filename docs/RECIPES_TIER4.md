Tier 4 (Singular) is the **singularity layer** (endgame):

- Introduces **Singularity, implants, endgame**.
- Has **4 machines** (Graviton Stabilizer, Superposed Reactor, Cyber Chamber, Nexus Supercomputer).
- Has **4 cables** (T4, upgraded).
- Has **1 electronic component** (Quantum Capacitor Singularis).

**Philosophy:** Tier 4 is the **endgame**. You can now create Stabilized Singularity, install implants, generate massive amounts of energy, and build a global network.
