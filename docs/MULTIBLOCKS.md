# QUANTA MOD - MULTIBLOCKS

> **Document based on QUANTA_MASTERLIST v10.6**
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-16
> **Scope:** Multiblock structures (Forge, Black Hole Forge, Graviton Stabilizer, etc.)

## 🎯 OVERVIEW

Multiblocks are **large structures** built from multiple blocks. They are the **endgame** of the Quanta Mod — used for advanced crafting, energy generation, and implants.

**Philosophy:** each multiblock is a **checkpoint** of progression. You can't craft tier 3+ materials without the Forge, or tier 4 without the Black Hole Forge.

## ⚡ 4. SUPERPOSED REACTOR (5×5×5 to 21×21×21)

### 4.1 Function

Massive **Quanta generation** (2.000-10.000+ Q/t).

### 4.2 Structure

- **Flexible size:** from 5×5×5 to 21×21×21.
- **Walls:** Reactor Casing.
- **Center:** Superposed Reactor Core.
- **Fuel:** Liquid Quanta/Chaos or Quanta Gas/Chaos Gas.

### 4.3 Construction

1. Build the outer shell (Reactor Casing).
2. Place the Superposed Reactor Core in the center.
3. Connect fuel pipes (Liquid or Gas).
4. Right-click the Core to open the GUI.

### 4.4 Detection

- The multiblock is **formed** when the shell is complete and the Core is in the center.
- The Core **glows** when formed.

### 4.5 Generation

| 9×9×9    | 4.000            |

## ⚛️ 6. PARTICLE COLLIDER (variable)

### 6.1 Function

Collides **materials** (generates knowledge and items).

### 6.2 Structure

- **Geometries:** Circular (Synchrotron) or Linear (LINAC).
- **Minimum:** radius 3 (circular) or 5 blocks (linear).
- **Blocks:** Quantum Collider Magnet, Particle Injector, Collision Chamber, Energy Input Port.

### 6.3 Construction

1. Build the ring (circular) or line (linear) with Quantum Collider Magnets.
2. Place Particle Injectors at the ends.
3. Place the Collision Chamber in the center.
4. Connect Energy Input Ports.
5. Right-click the Collision Chamber to open the GUI.

### 6.4 Detection

- The multiblock is **formed** when the ring/line is complete.
- The Collision Chamber **glows** when formed.

### 6.5 Function

- Multiple injectors (ternary, quaternary fusion, etc.).
- Collision Chamber in different locations changes results.
- Particles are always cyan (`#00F5FF`) regardless of material.
- **Does not generate energy** — only knowledge and items.
