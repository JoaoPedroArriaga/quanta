# QUANTA MOD - DECORATIONS

> **Document based on QUANTA_MASTERLIST v10.6**
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-16
> **Scope:** Decorative blocks in Quanta Mod

## 🎯 OVERVIEW

Quanta Mod has **decorative blocks** for every stage of progression:

- **Structural:** metal blocks, bricks, pillars.
- **Glass:** panels, stained glass, grilles.
- **Lighting:** lamps, crystals, light beams.
- **Industrial:** scaffolds, coils, pipes.
- **Magic-high-tech:** altars, ritual circles, lenses.

**Philosophy:** decorative blocks follow the **color palette of each Tier**. Players can build themed bases.

## ⚠️ 7. BALANCING NOTES

- **Structural Blocks:** use 9 materials (using vanilla).
- **Glass:** use 8 materials + 1 dye.
- **Lighting:** use 4 materials + 1 light source.
- **Industrial:** use 4 materials + 1 component.
- **Magic/High-Tech:** use 8 materials + 1 special component.

**Rule:** decorative blocks are **cosmetic** (they have no mechanical function).
