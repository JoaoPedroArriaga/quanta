# QUANTA MOD - UPGRADE KITS

> **Document based on QUANTA_MASTERLIST v10.6**
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-16
> **Scope:** Upgrade Kits for machines, cables, tools, and armor

## 🎯 OVERVIEW

**Upgrade Kits** are items used to **increase the tier** of machines, cables, tools, and armor. Unlike **Addons** (temporary), Upgrade Kits are **permanent**.

**Philosophy:** the player applies an Upgrade Kit to a machine, cable, tool, or armor piece to increase its tier. The tier determines:

- **Machines:** addon slots, speed, unlocked functions.
- **Cables:** throughput.
- **Tools:** durability, damage, modules.
- **Armor:** protection, module slots, Q capacity.

## 🏭 3. HOW TO USE UPGRADE KITS

### 3.1 Machines

- Open the machine's GUI.
- Place the Upgrade Kit in the upgrade slot.
- The tier is **permanently increased**.
- The machine gains **more addon slots** and **better performance**.

### 3.2 Cables

- Right-click the cable with the Upgrade Kit.
- The cable is **upgraded** (throughput increases).
- The cable's name **does not change** (only its tier).

### 3.3 Tools

- Open the tool's GUI (or use the Quantum Upgrade Table).
- Place the Upgrade Kit in the upgrade slot.
- The tool is **upgraded** (durability, damage, modules).
- **Preserves modules and enchantments**.

### 3.4 Armor

- Open the armor's GUI (or use the Quantum Upgrade Table).
- Place the Upgrade Kit in the upgrade slot.
- The armor is **upgraded** (protection, module slots, Q).
- **Preserves modules and enchantments**.

## ⚠️ 6. BALANCING NOTES

- **Decoherent Kit:** available at T0 (cheap).
- **Entangled Kit:** available at T1 (moderate).
- **Superposed Kit:** available at T2 (expensive).
- **Singular Kit:** available at T3 (very expensive).

**Rule:** each kit uses **components from the previous tier** (clear progression).
